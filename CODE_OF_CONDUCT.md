# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate with technical clarity and respect. Discussion is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project events.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Serious incidents may be reported privately to the maintainers through the repository contact channel.
